The data problem

The model field is empty.

CPSC’s structured Products[].Model field is populated in 0% of the measured records. Reclume recovers model, SKU, lot, style, serial, and item identifiers from recall prose before building the benchmark.

Preliminary, by design

01

Identifier precision has an AI first pass, not a human gold set. Recall is still unmeasured.

02

Code-based negatives are synthetic. They are absent from every recall record, but may not be real catalogue products.

03

Rule scoring needs human validation. Model runs are research previews, not final leaderboard claims.
